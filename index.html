Why this work

The strongest part of my profile isn't one technology. It's the overlap between security, AI and systems that matter.

I spent the last year building and shipping an AI system for the energy sector. At i3Technology, an IBM Silver Partner, I led development of GridSentinel AI — a five-agent platform for East African grid management.

I later presented the work directly to TANESCO, Tanzania's national electricity utility, with IBM Lab Services in attendance, securing a proof-of-concept commitment within two weeks.

That experience shaped the direction I am pursuing: security for intelligent systems operating in real environments. My foundation in IBM QRadar SIEM/SOAR/EDR, digital forensics, cybercrime investigation and full-stack development gives me a cross-disciplinary way of approaching that problem.
